About G2 Health Campaign

Empowering communities through accessible healthcare guidance

Our Mission & Vision

The 9C Boys G2 Health Campaign is an initiative dedicated to bridging the gap between individuals and professional medical diagnostics. We provide seamless registration tokens, direct specialist doctor assignments (including general medicine, ophthalmology, orthopedics, dietology, dermatology, and cardiology), and structured camp scheduling across major cities in India.

Designed and powered exclusively by @9C Boys G2, our portal ensures quick access, secure local browser record-keeping, and organized time-slot management to make community health camps efficient for everyone.
